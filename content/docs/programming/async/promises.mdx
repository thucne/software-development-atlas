## TL;DR

A Promise represents an eventual outcome. Calling `then`, `catch`, or `finally` creates a new Promise; the new Promise's outcome is determined by what the handler returns, throws, or adopts.

**Resolved does not necessarily mean fulfilled.** A promise can already be resolved to another still-pending promise or thenable and therefore remain pending while it adopts that outcome.
